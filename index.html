Take 15 demo trades while following the system perfectly. This phase is not about making money — it is about building the habit of following rules every single time.

📆 Week-by-Week Breakdown
Week 1: Learning the Process (2-3 trades)
Focus: Getting comfortable with the workflow. Expect most days to have no valid setup. That's normal and correct.
Day 1: Set everything up. Maybe take 1 trade. Day 2: Check for setup. Probably nothing valid. Day 3: Find 60% setup → Skip it (below 65%). Day 4: Find 68% setup → Take it! First demo trade! Day 5: Nothing valid. That's fine. Day 6-7: Weekend. Review what happened.
Week 2: Building Rhythm (3-4 more trades, total 5-7)
Focus: Speed up the process. Getting faster at reading TradingView and filling the website. Still making mistakes - that's OK.
Week 3: Refining (4-5 more trades, total 9-12)
Focus: Quality over quantity. Notice patterns in your winning vs losing trades. Adjust if win rate below 40% (raise score threshold to 75%).
Week 4: Final Push (reach 15 total trades)
Focus: Proving consistency. 15 trades, 50%+ win rate, 90%+ discipline = ready for Phase 2.

⚠️ Common Phase 1 Mistakes
  • ❌ Taking trades below 65% → "Close enough" kills accounts
  • ❌ Skipping psychology check → Takes 30 seconds, DO IT
  • ❌ Watching the trade → Set it and FORGET it
  • ❌ Moving stop loss → NEVER. It's set for a reason.
  • ❌ Revenge trading → Loss lock prevents this. Trust it.
  • ❌ Trading outside the 5-pair list → only USDCAD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, NZDUSD.
  • ❌ Using lower timeframes → 1H chart for entries. Period.
✅ The 5 Rules for Phase 1
  • ✅ ONE trade per day maximum
  • ✅ Follow the checklist EVERY time (no shortcuts)
  • ✅ Accept losses without emotion (planned 0.5–1% risk is OK)
  • ✅ Journal every single day (even no-trade days)
  • ✅ Never skip the daily checklist
❓ Phase 1 FAQ
Can't find 15 trades in 30 days?
Take longer. Phase 1 can take 4-8 weeks. Quality over speed. Patience IS the skill you're building.
Win rate below 50%?
Review which trades lost. Check mistake tracker. Raise score threshold to 75%. Adjust ONE thing at a time.
Can I trade other pairs?
Only the approved 5 pairs: USDCAD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, NZDUSD. No metals, crypto, indices, or extra FX pairs.
Can I use 15-minute chart?
NO. 1H chart for entries. Lower timeframes = more noise = more losses.

💰 Real Money Rules
Account: $284 practice account Risk per trade: 0.5–1% of account Max trades per day: 1 Max trades per week: 3 Pairs: USDCAD / EURUSD / GBPUSD / USDJPY / AUDUSD / NZDUSD only Min score: 65% Min RR: 1:4 Stop after ANY loss for the day
📅 Week-by-Week Plan
Week 5-6: First Real Trades (4-6 trades)
Focus: Managing emotions with real money. You WILL feel different. Accept it. Follow the SAME process as demo. The fear is normal.
What changes with real money: - Your heart will beat faster - You'll want to check more often - Losses will feel worse - Wins will feel amazing - You'll be tempted to increase risk What must NOT change: - Your process (identical to demo) - Your risk (0.5–1% max) - Your rules (all of them) - Your patience (same waiting) - Your journaling (every trade)
Week 7-8: Building Confidence (4-6 more trades)
Focus: Trusting yourself under pressure. By now you should have 8-12 real trades. The process should feel natural. Emotional reactions should be decreasing.
📊 Expected Results
Best case (consistent execution): 8-12 trades taken 5-7 wins × $6.00 = +$30-42 3-5 losses × planned risk = controlled drawdown Net: +$22.50 to +$34.50 Account: $72-84 Average case (some mistakes): 8-12 trades taken 4-6 wins × $6.00 = +$24-36 4-6 losses × planned risk = controlled drawdown Net: +$15 to +$27 Account: $65-77 Worst case (too many mistakes): Account below $45 Action: STOP. Go back to demo. Fix mistakes. Try again.

  • ❌ Taking more trades because "I'm on a streak" → STICK TO 1/DAY
  • ❌ Skipping psychology check because "I know the system" → ALWAYS DO IT
  • ❌ Trading when emotional about real money → FOLLOW MOOD CHECK
  • ❌ Comparing to YouTube traders → FOCUS ON YOUR OWN DATA

  • ✅ Top 2 mistakes reduced or eliminated
📖 Key Mindset for Phase 2
"When you genuinely accept the risks, you will be at peace with any outcome." — Mark Douglas, Trading in the Zone "The most important rule of trading is to play great defense, not offense." — Paul Tudor Jones, Market Wizards Real money changes nothing about your system. It only changes your emotions. Your job: Follow the same process regardless.
❓ Phase 2 FAQ
What if I lose money?
Small losses are NORMAL. A 0.5–1% loss is survivable and planned. Stay calm and keep the sample size clean.
Should I increase risk if winning?
NO. Stay at 0.5–1% risk. The risk percentage stays the same. As account grows, the dollar amount grows naturally.
What if I go below $40?
STOP real trading. Go back to demo for 10 more trades. Fix what's wrong. Then try real money again. No shame in this.
Can I add another market?
No. Stay inside the approved 5 forex pairs only until the data proves consistency.

📈 Growth Rules
Risk: Stay at 0.5–1% of account RR: Stay at 1:4 Frequency: 2-3 trades per week Pair: USDCAD primary Optional: Add EURUSD as secondary (after 40+ trades) As account grows, risk dollar amount grows: $65 × 1% = $0.65 risk $85 × 1% = $0.85 risk $100 × 1% = $1.00 risk $150 × 1% = $1.50 risk Same percentage, bigger positions, bigger wins.
📅 Month-by-Month Projection
Month 3: Consolidation ($65 → $85)
Focus: Consistent execution. Don't change anything that works. Consider adding EURUSD if USDCAD mastered. Keep discipline at 95%+.
Month 4-6: Steady Growth ($85 → $150)
Focus: Compounding. At $150, 1% risk = $1.50, and 1:4 wins = $6. Each winning trade is meaningful without oversized risk. Don't let larger numbers affect your psychology.
Month 3-6: Scaling ($350 → $500)
Focus: Patience and consistency. At $500, consider reducing risk to 2% for safety. Each trade risks $10 and wins $40. This is real money now.
Year 2: Professional Level ($500 → $2,000+)
Focus: Treating trading as a business. Track monthly returns. Consider funding challenges. Reduce risk to 1-2%. Consistency is everything.
🎯 Adding a Second Pair
When to add EURUSD: ✅ 40+ trades on USDCAD ✅ Consistent 50%+ win rate ✅ Account at $100+ ✅ Feel comfortable with USDCAD How to add: 1. Add Forex Auto Scorer to EURUSD chart 2. Study EURUSD for 1 week (no trades) 3. Take 5 demo trades on EURUSD 4. If 50%+ win rate → Add to real trading 5. Max 1 trade per day TOTAL (either pair) Do NOT add more than 2 pairs until $500+ account.
